Where to find .insgps files

When you record GPS data with the Insta360 app (using the GPS Dashboard feature), the app saves .insgps files to your phone's storage at:

  • Android: Android/data/com.arashivision.insta360akiko/files/Insta360/phone_gps/
  • iOS: Use the Insta360 app's file export, or browse the app's Documents folder via Files.

The filename (e.g. 1770835811829.insgps) is the Unix timestamp in milliseconds of when the recording started.
